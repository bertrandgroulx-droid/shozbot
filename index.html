iPhone or iPad. Open the app in Safari, tap the Share button — the square with an arrow coming out of it — then choose Add to Home Screen.

Android. Open the app in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then choose Install app. Older versions call it Add to home screen.

It is still just the website — nothing is downloaded, and no app store is involved. You get the icon on your home screen, and the app opens full screen without the address bar.
